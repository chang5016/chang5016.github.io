## GridForge for Unity

The browser-native city spatial grid in `app/gridforge-city-grid.ts` adapts the
publicly documented deterministic sparse-grid architecture from
[GridForge for Unity](https://github.com/mrdav30/GridForge-Unity), rather than
embedding a Unity runtime in the website.
