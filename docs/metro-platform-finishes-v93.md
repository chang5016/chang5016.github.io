# Complete platform finishes

All six platforms retain the original downloaded canopy, concrete feet, closed platform slab, native door leaves and pockets, bench slats, handrails, light housings and display enclosures. The v92 terrazzo paving and grounded hall roof remain in place.

The platform sides and concrete feet now have a full photographic concrete colour, normal and roughness material. Canopy undersides and bench slats share finished oak. The roof sheet, branched steel supports and recessed pocket infills use painted metal. Handrails, door frames, door pocket frames, overhead headers and light/display housings have a separate metal finish. Native lamp diffusers have a warm neutral surface instead of the old reddish plastic map.

Source triangles retain their original positions, normals and provenance. Metric UVs are projected on horizontal and vertical faces after fitting, so narrow fittings, 25-metre supports and long platform sides do not stretch a single image. Platform railings use full original sections at spans no longer than 8 m, with a 1.15 m top rail. The lift openings and boarding apertures remain clear. The opaque door recess is retained to conceal retracted door leaves.

Colour textures use sRGB; OpenGL normal and roughness textures remain linear. The four finish families share their 2K images across all stations, use repeat wrapping and 8× anisotropy, and are included in the existing static spatial material batches. Existing train, lift and city surfaces are preserved.

- [Metal 032, Lennart Demes, ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Metal032), fitted at a 0.5 m repeat.

Exact download URLs, original file sizes and SHA-256 checksums are retained in `public/models/metro/downloaded/platform-finish-sources.json` and `aluminium-source.json`. Visible metro geometry remains sourced from the pinned Libre-TrainSim downloads; no generated primitive geometry was introduced.
