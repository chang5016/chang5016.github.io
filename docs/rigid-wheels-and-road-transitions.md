# Rigid wheel and expressway revision

Replace the original front/rear wheel triangle volumes with independent axle groups, round rubber tires, alloy rims, hubs, brake discs, spokes and bolts. Rotation is rigid about local Z; front steering and suspension act on the axle. Disable the original-body wheel deformation uniforms. Preserve the original source asset and body textures. Wheel-volume removal is a geometric approximation and requires visual inspection at the fender boundaries; no cover-image parity or GPU acceptance is claimed.

Four mainline connecting routes and the river bridge retain 16m road width. Branch ramps flare to 10.8m near their elevated connections and taper smoothly to their 7.2m middle over up to 90m. Deck, underside, fascia, rail and edge stripes use matching endpoint widths. Elevation support interpolates the same widths. Bridge beams now slope with their deck rather than remaining horizontal at the segment's mean height. Guardrails open at height-matched ramp connections.

Native Node checks confirm smooth ramp widths and unchanging 16m mainline profiles. Local dependency installation remains unavailable, so publication uses the source-build path. Full route driving and rendered wheel inspection remain unverified.
