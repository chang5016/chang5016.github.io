# Supported station roofs and metric platform finishes

The previous civic canopy contained two disconnected roof volumes from the original downloaded building. The lower roof was also sized against the larger upper roof's bounds, leaving its eaves short of the curtain wall. The hall now fits those original volumes separately: a 47 × 111 m primary roof and an 18.8 × 30.8 m raised daylight lantern.

The original roof triangles and timber trim remain. A real opening in the lower roof leads into the lantern; whole original framed windows close the lantern's sides and follow the measured lower pitch. Perimeter columns, continuous transfer girders, transverse members and fitted pitched rafters connect the floors, facade and both roof volumes. The entry canopy has four grounded posts and connected eave/crosshead beams. Supports omit the railway and road entrance passages. Every visible component retains its downloaded geometry source and hash; no new primitive shell is used.

Platforms retain their closed structural slabs and boarding datum. Shared instances of original closed source panels form approximately 600 mm paving, with 2 mm supported joints and a 12 mm maximum finish above the slab. Yellow edge-warning and blue wayfinding courses have fixed positions. The hall and lift floors use the same physical terrazzo finish with world-scale UVs. Existing platform canopies retain their complete posts, arms, lamps and roof surfaces; structural steel and roof textures now have consistent finishes and scale.

The terrazzo colour, OpenGL normal and roughness files are unmodified 2K downloads from [Terrazzo Tiles, Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/terrazzo_tiles), by Amal Kumar, CC0. Original URLs, byte counts and SHA-256 hashes are in `public/models/metro/downloaded/terrazzo-source.json`. Geometry adaptation and licence attribution remain in `sources.json` and the supplied Libre-TrainSim licence notices. Reproduce the fitted architecture from the retained source GLBs with `node scripts/metro-import/finish-architecture-v92.mjs`.

Validation uses actual shipped geometry: roof support continuity, open daylight volume and glazed enclosure; supported paving and boarding height; unchanged static instance uploads; source hashes; all six elevator return routes and physical door clearances; all six recurring AI metro riders plus both intercity riders. Focused native Dawn/WebGPU renders decode the actual textures and CJK font at exterior, roof, lobby and platform viewpoints. They are internal rendering checks, not cloud-browser acceptance or a player-device FPS measurement. Static paving shares geometry/materials and uses three draws per visible platform rather than thousands of individual tile objects.
